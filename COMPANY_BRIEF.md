### 📖 Who They Are

Heaven Furniture Mart is one of Chattogram's leading bespoke furniture brands. They design and craft custom furniture — sofas, beds, dining sets, office pieces — built around what a customer actually wants, not pulled off a shelf. 

Their pitch to the market is simple: **"Designed. Crafted. Customized."**

> **Goal:** The landing page should feel like walking into a luxury interior studio — not an online furniture shop.

---

### 🛋️ What They Sell

* **Living Room:** Sofas, coffee tables, TV units, consoles
* **Bedroom:** Beds, wardrobes, dressing tables, bedside tables
* **Dining:** Dining tables, dining chairs, cabinets
* **Office & Study:** Executive tables, bookshelves, workstations
* **Bespoke / Custom:** Anything custom-built to space, size, and personal taste

### 🎨 The Brand Vibe & Design Direction

> The page should feel like entering a luxury furniture and interior studio.

* **Aesthetic:** Warm, editorial, spacious, confident — letting photography speak.
* **Avoid:** Cheap, crowded, marketplace look, generic templates, loud colors, walls of text.

#### Suggested Color Palette:
* **Deep Charcoal-Teal:** Grounding dark background
* **Warm Ivory:** Clean space around content
* **Muted Gold / Brass:** Small accents (from logo)
* **Deep Brown:** Text and grounding tones
* **Natural Wood Tan:** Material-led accents

#### Typography:
* **Headlines:** Elegant Serif
* **Body / Buttons / Details:** Clean Modern Sans-Serif

### 🏗️ Recommended Landing Page Structure

1. **Hero Section:** One strong image/headline (e.g., *"Furniture, Crafted Around You"*) + primary CTA.
2. **Brand Intro:** 2–3 concise sentences introducing bespoke furniture & interior styling from Chattogram.
3. **Why Choose Heaven:** Quick-to-scan trust value props.
4. **Collections Snapshot:** Curated category cards (Living, Bedroom, Dining, Bespoke).
5. **Bespoke Highlight:** Dedicated showcase for their #1 differentiator.
6. **Social Proof & Credibility:** MD quote, client satisfaction stats, showroom imagery, milestones.
7. **One Clear Call-To-Action (CTA):** Prominent "Request a Quote" or "WhatsApp Us" button.
8. **Footer:** Showroom address, phone, email, and social links.

### 🏆 Judging & Evaluation Criteria

* **Luxury Studio Feel:** Does it feel like high-end luxury rather than a generic catalogue? (Top priority)
* **Immediate Brand Clarity:** Is the brand identity clear within 30 seconds?
* **Mobile Responsiveness:** Flawless mobile presentation and performance.
* **Conversion Focus:** Clear, frictionless path to single primary action.
* **Performance & Polish:** Fast load times, clean code, no clutter.
